Rules for Gatos & Cães (Cats & Dogs)
8×8 board.
Players take turns placing ONE piece (Cat or Dog).
Cats go first.
The first Cat must go on one of the 4 centre squares.
The first Dog must go OUTSIDE the centre squares.
You can never place a Cat next to a Dog (↑↓←→), or a Dog next to a Cat.
The player who places the LAST piece wins.
If you have no legal squares on your turn, you LOSE.
