Rules for Quelhas (Misère segments)
10×10 board.
The Vertical player places VERTICAL segments (at least 2 squares).
The Horizontal player places HORIZONTAL segments (at least 2 squares).
The player with the Vertical direction goes first.
Click the starting square, then the end square to place a segment.
Segments occupy consecutive empty squares.
Swap rule: after the first move, the Horizontal player may swap roles.
WARNING: this is a MISÈRE game. Whoever makes the last move loses!
If you have no moves on your turn, you WIN (your opponent was the last to play).

Tip: think carefully before each move!
