Rules for Atari Go
9×9 board (play on the intersections).
Black goes first.
Group: stones of the same colour connected vertically or horizontally.
Liberty: an empty intersection next to a group.
A group with 0 liberties is captured and removed.
Suicide is not allowed, except when capturing opponent stones.
GOAL: the first player to make ANY capture WINS!
