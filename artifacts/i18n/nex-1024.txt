Learning path

championship practice

Current focus: Shorten your connection distance and use the neutral piece to block your opponent's path.

Mistake to avoid: Placing your own piece well but wasting the neutral piece without blocking the best opponent route.

Discover

• Understand which sides each colour connects.
• Distinguish the roles of your own piece and the neutral piece in a move.

Challenge: Beat L1 once.

Tactics

• Build bridges and double connection threats.
• Place neutral pieces on cutting squares in your opponent's path.

Challenge: Beat L2 twice in a row.

Strategy

• Create three immediate connection threats and check that your opponent cannot win first.
• Use replacement when the converted neutral pieces complete your connection.

Challenge: Beat L3 and then L4, once each.

Championship

• Choose placement or replacement based on the available connection.
• Review two losses and find the wasted neutral piece.

Challenge: Win at least half your games against L4.

Rules for Nex
Diamond-shaped hexagonal grid (11×11 squares).
Black connects the ↖ upper-left and lower-right ↘ sides.
White connects the ↗ upper-right and lower-left ↙ sides.
On each turn, choose ONE action:
• Placement: 1 of your own pieces + 1 neutral (grey)
• Replacement: 2 neutral→own + 1 own→neutral
Pie rule: after the first move, White may swap colours.
The first player to connect their two sides wins!

💡
Tip: think carefully before each move!
