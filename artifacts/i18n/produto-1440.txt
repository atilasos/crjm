Learning path

championship practice

Current focus: Create two strong groups of your own and reduce your opponent's product whenever possible.

Mistake to avoid: Making one giant group and ending up with a product of zero.

Discover

• The opening places one piece; later turns place two, in colours you choose.
• Know that the score multiplies the two largest groups.

Challenge: Beat L1 once.

Tactics

• Assess opponent merges by comparing products before and after.
• Protect the second group: without it, the product drops to zero.

Challenge: Beat L2 twice in a row.

Strategy

• Prefer moves with two effects: growth and a threat.
• At the end, strengthen the smaller factor and use the opponent's colour as a wall.

Challenge: Beat L3 and then L4, once each.

Championship

• Balance your two largest groups until the last turn.
• Review two losses and find the decisive merge or separation.

Challenge: Win at least half your games against L4.

Rules for Produto (Product)
Hexagonal board with sides of 5 cells.
On each turn, place TWO pieces on empty cells.
IMPORTANT: you can place pieces of ANY colour, yours or your opponent's!
Exception: on the first move, Black places only ONE piece.
Score: (largest group) × (second-largest group).
If you have only 1 group, your score is ZERO.
Tiebreak: the player with fewer pieces on the board wins.
The game ends when the board is full.
