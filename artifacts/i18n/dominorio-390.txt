Rules for Dominório (Domineering)
8×8 board.
The Vertical player places VERTICAL dominoes (2 squares).
The Horizontal player places HORIZONTAL dominoes (2 squares).
Vertical goes first.
Each domino occupies two adjacent empty squares.
The player who places the LAST piece wins.
If you have no moves on your turn, you LOSE.
